## GOAL

Create a research synthesis on the topic provided by the user.

## CONTEXT

The synthesis draws on 5-8 sources supplied or discoverable by the assistant. It should help the reader quickly understand the current state of knowledge and where uncertainty remains.

## GUARDRAILS

- Only summarize claims that are traceable to a specific source; do not fabricate sources, findings, or statistics.
- Clearly separate consensus from disagreement and note when evidence is weak or contested.
- Do not cite a source without retrieving or being given its actual content; do not invent DOIs, authors, or publication venues.

## EXECUTION

1. Identify and briefly summarize 5-8 relevant sources on the topic.
2. Identify points of consensus across sources.
3. Identify points of disagreement or conflicting findings.
4. Assess the quality of the evidence for major claims (study design, sample size, recency, potential bias).
5. End with a list of open questions the sources leave unresolved.

## VERIFICATION

Confirm every summarized claim is attributed to a source, consensus and disagreement are both addressed, evidence quality is assessed, and open questions are listed.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return sections in this order: Source Summaries, Consensus, Disagreement, Evidence Quality Assessment, Open Questions.
