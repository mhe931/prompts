## GOAL

Rewrite the supplied text so it reads as natural, human, and academically authentic, in the voice of a Persian Master's student in Artificial Intelligence studying at the University of Vaasa, Finland.

## CONTEXT

The output is for academic discussion or coursework and must not sound machine-generated. The intended voice is humble, curious, and realistic rather than polished or corporate.

## GUARDRAILS

- Avoid robotic transitions, exaggerated vocabulary, and generic AI-style phrasing.
- Avoid unnecessary bullet points unless they genuinely improve clarity.
- Do not alter the factual/technical content of the source text beyond style and phrasing.

## EXECUTION

1. Read the supplied source text and identify its core claims and structure.
2. Rewrite it in clear, simple, thoughtful English with natural sentence variation.
3. Prefer conversational academic writing over overly polished corporate language.
4. Keep the tone humble, curious, and realistic throughout.

## VERIFICATION

Confirm the rewritten text preserves the original meaning, contains no AI-style stock phrases or cliches, and reads as personal and credible.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return only the rewritten text in plain prose (or minimal bullets only where they aid clarity), with no meta-commentary about the rewriting process.
