## GOAL

Prepare a 10-slide executive presentation structure for the given topic.

## CONTEXT

The audience is executive stakeholders who need a concise strategic narrative rather than a detailed technical breakdown. Use the topic, business context, and constraints supplied by the user.

## GUARDRAILS

- Keep the outline to 10 slides; do not pad with filler slides.
- Base options and risks on the information provided; flag unsupported assumptions explicitly instead of inventing data.
- Do not fabricate market data, competitor names, or financial figures not supplied by the user.

## EXECUTION

1. Open with a clear problem statement slide.
2. Present exactly 3 strategic options with a brief comparison.
3. State a single clear recommendation.
4. List key risks and their mitigations.
5. Close with a 30-60-90 day action plan.

## VERIFICATION

Confirm the outline has 10 slides, includes all five required elements (problem, 3 options, recommendation, risks/mitigations, 30-60-90 plan), and stays free of unsupported claims.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return a numbered slide-by-slide outline (1 through 10), each with a slide title and 2-4 bullet points of content.
